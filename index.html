How this stays up to date

Every series is pulled from the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis FRED database, which mirrors the BLS, BEA, Treasury, Census, Federal Reserve, Eurostat, and World Bank releases. A scheduled job fetches all of them on the 1st and 15th of each month and republishes this page. The button in the top right runs that same job on demand. The catalog of series lives in one file, catalog.json, so adding a dashboard is adding a few lines there.
